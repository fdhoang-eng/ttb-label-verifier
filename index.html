AI-Powered Alcohol Label Verification

TTB Compliance Prototype — Fast visual matching for brand, ABV, warning statement & more

1. Upload Label Image(s)

Upload one or more photos of the alcohol label. Clear, well-lit images work best.

2. Enter Application Data

Enter the values from the COLA / application form. These will be compared against the label.

If left empty, the tool checks the label against the official legal wording. If you fill this in, it will compare the label to your text instead.

3. Verification Results

Analyzing label with OCR + AI matching… This usually takes 2–8 seconds.

Prototype for Treasury / TTB Compliance Division. Not for production use.

Uses client-side OCR (Tesseract.js). No images leave your browser unless you choose to deploy with a backend.
